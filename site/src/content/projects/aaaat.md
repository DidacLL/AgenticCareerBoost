AAAAT is the separate Agent-Agnostic Auto Application Tracker project.

It continues the application-tracking direction first explored inside ACB, with the product work moved into its own repository rather than extending the ACB prototype indefinitely.

Its core direction is local-first storage for application data and tooling around reusable profile material and generated application artifacts.
