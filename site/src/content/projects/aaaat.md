AAAAT is a free, open-source desktop workspace for keeping original job offers and messages, finding application details when needed, and creating editable CVs and cover letters. The information lives in a folder chosen by the user. There is no mandatory application-stage workflow, cloud account, or AI dependency.

Optional AI helps with specific tasks, including extracting information and working on documents. I direct AAAAT's product and engineering decisions; its current implementation is AI-written, making the repository also a record of how I coordinate, review, and correct agent-assisted software development.
