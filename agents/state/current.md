# Current State

ACB is currently a personal career workspace and public proof record. It is not
running an active sprint or process framework.

## Public Surface

- Public site root: `https://didacll.github.io/`
- Source repository: `https://github.com/DidacLL/AgenticCareerBoost`
- The site, public CV, formal reports, and selected repository history are the
  parts intended for recruiter or colleague review.

## Local Surface

- `application-tracker/` remains a scratch/prototype workspace used locally
  until AAAAT and VCVGenerator are ready elsewhere.
- The simple cover-letter renderer is preserved for local use through
  `application-tracker/letter.ps1 <slug>`.
- Private application data, tailored letters/CVs, recruiter notes, raw offers,
  generated private PDFs, and local databases stay local and untracked.

## Historical Evidence

- `agents/state/logs/` keeps representative development logs.
- `agents/state/archive/` keeps low-signal logs that are preserved as history
  but are not part of the normal reading path.
- `agents/rules/`, `agents/tests/`, and old workflow notes are preserved as
  historical evidence, not current operating instructions.

## Current Direction

Future work in this repository should mainly improve the public website and
presentation. AAAAT and VCVGenerator belong to future/separate development, not
to this cleanup scope.
